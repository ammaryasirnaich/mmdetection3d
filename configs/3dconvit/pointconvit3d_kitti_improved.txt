What motivated these changes (from your scalars.json)
-----------------------------------------------------
From outputs/old_pointconvit3d_kitti/20260209_011023/vis_data/scalars.json:

1) Early training instability:
   - Very large early grad_norm spikes (e.g. >100 at the beginning) then it
     stabilizes later. This commonly benefits from LR warmup.

2) Score/objectness supervision looked ineffective:
   - The logged centerness_loss stays ~1e-6 for most of training, which implies
     the score head (`obj_scores`) receives almost no meaningful gradient.
   - This can hurt score calibration / ranking which impacts AP, especially when
     you later tune NMS/score thresholds.

3) LR schedule behavior:
   - Your original MultiStepLR drops at epochs 45 and 60. Loss continues to
     fluctuate/plateau later; cosine decay often improves the “last-mile”
     optimization.

New files created (no original files modified)
----------------------------------------------
1) New head implementation (fixes objectness supervision):
   - mmdet3d/models/dense_heads/ssd_3d_head_objness.py
   - It registers SSD3DHeadObjness via MODELS.register_module().
   - It changes only how obj_scores is supervised:
       * targets: explicit binary objectness targets (per-class channel)
       * weights: negatives weight 1, positives weight = centerness
     All other losses (center/dir/size/corner/vote) follow SSD3DHead behavior.

2) New config:
   - configs/3dconvit/pointconvit3d_kitti_improved.py

Summary of config changes vs base
---------------------------------
1) Custom import (to use the new head without editing package __init__.py):
   - custom_imports.imports = ['mmdet3d.models.dense_heads.ssd_3d_head_objness']

2) Head swap:
   - model.bbox_head.type = 'SSD3DHeadObjness'
   - model.bbox_head.objectness_loss.loss_weight = 2.0  (slightly stronger
     learning signal for scoring; you can tune 1.0–3.0)

3) Optimizer regularization:
   - AdamW weight_decay: 0.0  ->  0.01

4) LR schedule:
   - MultiStepLR -> (Linear warmup for 1 epoch) + CosineAnnealingLR
   - eta_min is set to 2e-5 to match the late-stage LR you previously reached.

5) Test-time NMS:
   - model.test_cfg.nms_cfg.iou_thr: 0.1 -> 0.25
     Note: 0.1 is very aggressive. For Car AP, sweeping 0.15/0.2/0.25/0.3 is
     often worthwhile.

6) Validation frequency (to see Car AP improvements sooner):
   - train_cfg.val_interval: 40 -> 10

2) grad_norm should be less spiky during the first epoch (due to warmup).
3) Car AP40 moderate/strict should improve or become more stable earlier.
